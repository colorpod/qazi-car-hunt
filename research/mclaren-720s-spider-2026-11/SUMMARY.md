# McLaren 720S Spider research, 2026-11-07

Found 31 public 2020 Spider records in the Autostoday aggregation plus additional dealer and auction records. Kept 7 VIN-identified candidates under 20,000 miles. The Houston 3,199-mile car was excluded because BidCars shows a Copart sale with front-end and undercarriage damage.

Best documented car: McLaren San Francisco, 6,969 miles at $269,991. Its free CARFAX shows one California personal owner, no accidents or title brands, 10 service records, and only one non-routine repair, interior trim repair.

Best value candidate: McLaren Chicago at $230,000 and 18,466 miles, but its clean title and CARFAX are unverified. European Exotic Center is the strongest alternate because its accessible CARFAX summary shows no accidents and 18 service records, with two owners.

Recent exact-model sales found: 3 in the last 12 months, median $254,007, range $240,000-$263,000. Direction is downward / softening. BaT and Mecum observations were not counted as sold when they were still live or no-sale.

Important gap: iPacket returned HTTP 403 for every VIN tested. Most free CARFAX detailed reports were rate-limited after the first full report, so the JSON and per-car notes explicitly preserve those unknowns.
