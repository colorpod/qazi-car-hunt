# McLaren 600LT Spider research

Research date: 2026-11-07. Target was any-year US McLaren 600LT Spider under 20,000 miles.

Seven current matching Spider records were identified from free public feeds. Six were kept for the evidence set. The strongest value is the 1 of 1 Motorsports car at $244,998 with 18,132 miles. The strongest documented factory-style specification is Chicago Motor Cars at $259,800 with 15,205 miles and an extensive option list.

Two exact 600LT Spider sales were found in the last 12 months: $201,000 on BaT and $225,000 after bidding on Cars & Bids. Median was $213,000, with a $201,000 to $225,000 range.

Free CARFAX URLs were captured for every kept car, but each report route presented a DataDome CAPTCHA. Owners, full records, repairs, auction history, and clean-title status are therefore held as unverified unless explicitly supported by a dealer or marketplace summary. No dealer was contacted and no form, purchase, sign-in, or paid report was submitted.
